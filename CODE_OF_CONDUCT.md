# Code of Conduct

We want Outention to be a constructive, technically rigorous project where people can disagree without harassment.

Be respectful, assume good faith, criticize ideas rather than people, and do not publish another person's private information. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its participants. Serious conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile.
