# 30-second demo

The core proof is not “AI summarizes social media.” It is that the same original sources become a different useful feed when the person states a different intention.

1. Show the source list and enter: `Thoughtful updates from people I follow — less news, more lived experience.`
2. Scroll through several original posts and briefly open **Why this?**
3. Replace the intention with: `Practical open-source AI projects and honest reports from people building them — no hype.`
4. Update the feed and show that the ranking, sources, and explanations change while every card remains an original post.
5. End on: **Tell your feeds what you want to hear today.**

Record with public or synthetic accounts only. Do not expose API keys, private handles, private posts, browser autofill, or local configuration paths containing a username.
